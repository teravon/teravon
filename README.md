# teravon

Oscar A. Virguez · Security research

I work on side-channel attacks: recovering secret keys from a chip by measuring
physical signals, such as its power consumption, while it runs cryptography. My
current research looks at how deep-learning models learn to do this and which parts
of the signal they rely on. I also do bug bounty on
[HackerOne](https://hackerone.com/teravon).

## Research

**[feature_emergence](https://github.com/teravon/feature_emergence)**: a
reproduction and extension of *You have to be Realistic: On Investigating Feature
Emergence in Deep Learning-based Side-channel Analysis* (Karayalçın et al., 2025).
It is written as an 8-chapter guide for readers new to the field, from power traces
and masking to key recovery on the ASCADr, ESHARD and CHES CTF datasets. Every
figure can be regenerated from the scripts in the repository.
